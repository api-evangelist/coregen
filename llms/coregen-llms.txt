## Science

- [The Science](https://www.coregen.com/thescience): Knocking out the SRC-3 gene in regulatory T (Treg) cells reprograms them from immunosuppressors into tumor-killing effectors; SRC family coactivators are the "master regulators" of gene function, researched by the O'Malley Lab at Baylor College of Medicine since the 1970s.
- [Preclinical Studies](https://www.coregen.com/preclinicalstudies): Preclinical results in tumor-bearing mice, reported by the company as eradication and prevention of tumors without observed toxicity or inflammation.
- [Pipeline](https://www.coregen.com/pipeline): Development programs, including CRG-150, an autologous CRISPR-engineered Treg cell therapy for advanced solid tumors.
- [Publications](https://www.coregen.com/publications): Peer-reviewed publications underpinning the platform; the 2023 Cozzarelli Prize was awarded for the company's founding science.
